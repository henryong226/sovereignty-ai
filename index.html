Slower, governed AI.

We think AI should move at the speed people can check. Safety and security are built in from the start. Every step stays under human oversight, and you decide what it does. We back clear rules and independent testing, not a race.

80% of US adults say the government should keep rules for AI safety and data security, even if AI develops more slowly.

Source: Gallup with the Special Competitive Studies Project, survey of 3,128 US adults, Apr–May 2025.

  1. 01.You stay in charge.

    AI suggests. A person decides. Anything that can't be undone waits for you. It works that way from the start, with no setting to find.

  2. 02.Seeing is understanding.

    If you can't see what AI did, you can't trust it, and you can't learn from it. Show the work, and people get better at the work.

  3. 03.Checked, then done.

    Nothing counts as done until someone has checked it. Skip the check and you've only moved the cleanup to later.

  4. 04.AI works for you, not instead of you.

    Let it take the busywork: the copying, the chasing, the formatting. You keep the work you're proud of. The goal is better days for the people doing the work.

  5. 05.Your data is yours.

    Your work, your context, your rules. You decide what AI can see and what it can touch.

  6. 06.Work should feel good.

    Progress you can see. A day that ends on time. Burnout comes from how work is designed, and tools can be designed better.

These beliefs build on the idea of AI sovereignty: control over your AI that you can see and prove. Further reading: What is AI sovereignty? (IBM Think)
